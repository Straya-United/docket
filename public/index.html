      From the ute to paid

      Dictate a job and Docket writes the quote from your price book. One tap sends it as a PayPal invoice with a link where your customer approves and pays the deposit. Extras found on site go through as a variation they approve. When you finish, they pay the balance on the same link.

      This is a demo on PayPal's sandbox, so no real money moves. Customer emails go to PayPal's sandbox, not to real inboxes.
